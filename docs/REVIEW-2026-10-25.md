# Design and UX review, 25 October 2026

Boxing Punch Quickness demo, Fight view first, Lab view untouched. Brief: booth use (striking from two to three metres, instant
comprehension, Simulate flawless), first screen = your bests plus Start a round, a punch that feels like something. Design, motion and
UX only: no change to the detector, thresholds, classifier, combo rules, the engagement rules or the CSV schema.

## Art direction

**A fight-night scoreboard.** Feels like the round timer on a gym wall: condensed numerals you can read from the other side of the
room, one hard hit per punch, everything else quiet.
**Masters:** Teenage Engineering (a display that is nearly all numerals, one accent), Mueller-Brockmann (a plain grid, rules not boxes).
**Signature: the impact slam.** The hand speed lands as the biggest thing on the page, in the colour of the punch type, with a ring
opening behind it and a wash along the card's edge. The colour stays after the motion, so it still reads under reduced motion.

Type pairing: a condensed display face for numerals and headlines (`DIN Condensed`, then `Bahnschrift Condensed`, `Barlow Condensed`,
`Roboto Condensed`, `Arial Narrow`; system faces only, so a booth with no network still gets one) over the system text face. Tabular
numerals everywhere a number moves. Colour roles unchanged: measured = teal, personal best = amber (the diamond), punch types keep
their four colours.

## Baseline (main at 6dcc122)

156 unit tests green. `npm run smoke:fun` green. Screens shot at 1280, 390 and 320 px, light and dark (`before-*` in the shots folder).

## Findings, ranked by impact

1. **The hero headline was a caption.** `.card h2` (small, grey, uppercase) beat `.hero-title`, so "Bag work, but it talks back." was 12 px
   and the line under it was bigger than the line itself. Fixed; a smoke check now fails if the headline drops under 32 px.
2. **Bests were nowhere on the first screen.** They sat at the bottom of the live cards, 940 px down at 1280, under four empty cards full
   of dashes (Rounds 0:30, Last Punch "-", Punch Count 0, Combos "-"). Now: hero, then the bests board, then only the timer settings.
   The empty punch cards stay away until there is a punch. Returning screen at 1280 x 800: Start a round and all four speed bests
   are above the fold (bottom of the row at 751 px; at 1280 x 720, 709 px).
3. **Nothing on the live stage could be read from a metre.** Call digits 48 px, hand speed 22 px, round time 12 px in a corner tag, the
   Stop button and Flow chip the same size as the speed. Now: round time 9 rem, call digits up to 9 rem, hand speed up to 12 rem (all
   tied to the viewport height as well as width, so the stage fits a 720 p projector), type and hand as large chips.
4. **A punch had almost no feel on the stage.** The number counted up from zero and a chip popped. Now: slam (scale and opacity,
   300 ms, spring), a ring that opens behind the number, an edge wash in the type colour, a four-step speed meter beside the tier chip
   (Easy 1 step, Snap 4), and a small bright tick after the thud on a Snap. The ring and wash fire at most three times a second
   however fast the punches come. Reduced motion: no motion at all, the colour, number, chip and meter stay.
5. **The time-left ring of a coach call never showed.** `show()` set `.hidden` on an SVG element, which has no such property, so the
   `hidden` attribute stayed. Fixed (`toggleAttribute`), with a smoke check.
6. **Sideways movement.** No root clip rule; the section links scrolled sideways at 320 px ("Sett..." cut off); the recap sheet was a
   hidden-scrollbar carousel. Now: `html, body { overflow-x: clip }`, section links wrap and fit at 320, the recap cards are stacked.
7. **Phone first screen.** Header 160 px (brand, tagline, three rows of controls), empty cards before the hero, headline wrapped
   mid-word ("warm-up"). Now: tagline dropped in Fight view on a phone (the hero says it), the speed label dropped under 420 px, the level
   bar one line, controls 44 px tall.
8. **Recap text.** Folded lines ran together ("Your longest chain 5 different combos this session"). Now a full stop between.
9. **The timer card duplicated the stage** while a round ran, and sat unused after it. It now shows with the hero only.

## Seen, not changed

- **Colour roles collide in two places.** The hook colour is amber (the personal-best role) and the cross colour is a periwinkle
  (the model role). Both predate the house roles and are used in tests and screenshots across the page; the shape cues (diamond for a
  best, words for types) keep them readable. Changing them is a decision for Jeff.
- **Simulate has to be tapped.** With no sensor and no Simulate, Start a round only shows a hint, so the teal button is
  "Simulate a round (no sensors)" and Start a round turns teal the moment a sensor or Simulate is live. A `?sim=1` kiosk link would
  make a booth one tap shorter; that is a feature, so it is left for a decision.
- **Bests from Simulate are demo-only.** The Simulate profile lives in memory (by design: nothing simulated enters a board), so a
  reload at the booth starts with an empty board. The board fills again within the first full round.
- **The kit's toasts and level-up modal** are vendored and unchanged; they sit over the top of the grade card for a moment.
- **Lab view** keeps its original look on purpose (it is the lab page as it was).

  hero's Start a round copy, the layout's punch count, the bests reaching the board with ids; one recap assertion added).
- `npm run smoke` (lab view) passes unchanged. `npm run smoke:fun` passes with 12 new checks: headline size, no empty cards on a first
  screen, the hit colour and the 64 px speed on the stage, the call ring showing, no slam/ring/wash under device reduced motion, the
  returning first screen (Start a round, four speed bests above the fold at 1280 x 800, m/s to mph on the board), 320 px with no sideways
  scroll and one 44 px row of section links.
- Overflow audited with a probe over hero, stage (with call, stamp and Count it forced on), recap, grade and bests, lab details and
  setup, at 320, 375 and 402 px, light and dark: no sideways scroll and no element outside the screen (the stamp's one-off entrance
  transform is clipped, not scrolled). Zero console errors or warnings in every run.
